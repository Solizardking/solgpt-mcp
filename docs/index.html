The Genesis of $CLAWD

            

                On March 31, 2025, a developer known as 8bit connected Claude to the Solana blockchain
                through a custom MCP server. The original conversation is replayed here — remade as
                $CLAWD.
            

            

                After checking the account balance of 0.142500891 SOL, fetching a price chart,
                scanning the top tokens on Pump.fun, and encountering several failed attempts due to
                image handling issues, the breakthrough came when 8bit provided a local image path.
            

            

                Claude processed the request, generated a mint keypair, constructed the token metadata,
                and broadcasted the transaction to Solana mainnet. In seconds,
                $CLAWD was live —
                mint 8cHzQHUS2s2h8TzCmfqPKYiM4dSt4roa3n7MyRLApump.
            

            

                The original Claude share
                45f926fb-1680-44aa-bd05-06ec48bdf23e
